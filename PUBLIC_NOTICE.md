# Public Disclosure, Intellectual Property, and Collaboration Notice

## 1. Purpose of this repository

This repository presents a public technical overview of the Cools thin-film III-V-on-SiC optical-semiconductor platform.

The disclosed concept includes, at a high level:

- transfer and bonding of an indium-phosphide-based III-V optically functional thin film onto a silicon-carbide-based support,
- separation of optical functionality from permanent thermal and mechanical support,
- donor recovery and reuse,
- representative bonding-interface and SiC-support variants,
- optional localized optical post-processing,
- and representative LiDAR, co-packaged-optics, SWIR, quantum-photonic, and photovoltaic applications.
