# Patent Portfolio Architecture

## Cools Thin-Film III-V on SiC Optical Semiconductor Platform

This document presents the public architectural map of the patent portfolio associated with transferring an indium-phosphide-based III-V optically functional thin-film layer onto a silicon-carbide-based support platform.

It is not a claim chart, legal opinion, filing-status representation, or substitute for the applicable patent specifications and claims.

---

## 1. Core platform axis

### Thin-film III-V optical functionality on a permanent SiC support

The central invention axis is the combination of:

1. a silicon-carbide-based support layer; and
2. an indium-phosphide-based III-V optically functional thin-film layer directly or indirectly bonded to the SiC support.

The III-V thin film provides optical functionality, while the SiC support provides thermal spreading and mechanical support.

The functional III-V layer may include:

- InP,
- InGaAs,
- InGaAsP,
- InAlGaAs,
- multiple-quantum-well stacks,
- laser stacks,
- APD or SPAD stacks,
- optical modulators,
- detectors,
- waveguides,
- or precursors of these devices.

The support is not limited to one SiC morphology. It may include single-crystal SiC, thin-film SiC on another carrier, polycrystalline SiC, or reaction-bonded SiC.

---

## 2. Functional separation axis

The portfolio separates the roles traditionally combined in a bulk InP wafer.

| Function | Assigned layer |
|---|---|
| Epitaxial and optical functionality | InP-based III-V donor and transferred thin film |
| Permanent heat-spreading path | SiC-based support |
| Mechanical handling and stiffness | SiC-based support |
| CTE matching | InP/SiC material pair and interface design |
| Package base and optical alignment | SiC-based support or composite SiC platform |
| Optional post-transfer optical treatment | Selected absorption region and controlled illumination path |

This separation is central to both the structural and manufacturing claims.

---

## 3. Transfer and donor-reuse axis

A key manufacturing axis includes:

- preparation of an InP-based III-V donor or donor stack,
- formation of a buried separation plane by hydrogen and/or helium implantation,
- bonding to the SiC-based support,
- splitting and transfer of the III-V functional thin film,
- finishing or device completion,
- and recovery and reuse of the remaining donor.

The donor may be transferred as:

- a bare optical-material layer,
- an epitaxial stack,
- a partially processed device stack,
- or a front-end-completed optical-device stack.

This axis protects the change from a consumable bulk III-V substrate to a reusable source of functional films.

---

## 4. SiC support variants

### 4.1 Single-crystal SiC support

Provides high thermal conductivity, crystalline uniformity, and potential rear-side optical access.

### 4.2 Thin-film SiC support

Allows SiC to be inserted between a III-V detector stack and another functional carrier, including a silicon CMOS readout integrated circuit.

### 4.3 Polycrystalline SiC support

Provides thermal conductivity and rigidity without requiring a full single-crystal support wafer.

### 4.4 Reaction-bonded SiC support

Provides large-area, lower-cost, high-stiffness thermal support. Optical access, where required, may be provided by:

- an opening,
- a local single-crystal SiC window,
- an optical via,
- a transparent insert,
- side illumination,
- or front illumination.

The portfolio does not require RBSC to behave as a uniformly transparent optical window.

## 5. Bonding-interface axis

The bonding interface may include one or more of:

- hydrophilic direct bonding,
- plasma-activated bonding,
- oxide-mediated bonding,
- nitride-mediated bonding,
- metal bonding,
- benzocyclobutene bonding,
- or an amine-containing molecular interface, including PEI- or PEIE-derived layers.

The interface may additionally provide:

- low-temperature joining,
- reduced interface thermal resistance,
- work-function modification,
- electrical contact control,
- stress accommodation,
- or chemical adhesion.

The core platform is not limited to one specific interface chemistry.

---

## 6. Thermal-expansion and interface-reliability axis

The InP/SiC material combination is selected to reduce mismatch during bonding, cooling, operation, and thermal cycling.

Portfolio embodiments may define:

- effective CTE-difference ranges,
- thin-film thickness ranges,
- interface thickness ranges,
- support-layer thickness ranges,
- low-temperature bonding conditions,
- and interface thermal-resistance requirements.

This axis distinguishes the platform from an InP thin film transferred onto a mechanically or thermally unsuitable carrier.

---

## 7. Optional optical post-processing axis

Optical post-processing is an optional extension of the platform rather than a mandatory limitation of the core architecture.

Possible illumination paths include:

- front-side illumination,
- rear-side illumination through the SiC support,
- side illumination,
- an optical via,
- or a transparent window.

Possible selective absorption regions include:

- multiple-quantum-well regions,
- doped regions,
- free-carrier absorption regions,
- implantation-damaged regions,
- defect-level regions,
- buried metal absorbers,
- interface reaction layers,
- and dedicated optical absorber layers.

Potential processes include:

- transfer-damage recovery,
- dopant activation,
- wavelength trimming,
- and local quantum-well intermixing.

For RBSC embodiments, the illumination path may be separately engineered rather than assuming uniform rear-side transparency.

## 8. Vertical optical, electrical, and thermal integration axis

The SiC support may include:

- an optical via,
- an electrical through-substrate via,
- a local transparent window,
- a vertical optical interconnect,
- or combined electrical/optical routing structures.

This enables the same permanent SiC platform to perform three-dimensional integration across optical, electrical, and thermal directions.

## 9. Device and application axes

### 9.1 Automotive LiDAR

Application embodiments include:

- 1,500–1,600 nm light sources,
- detectors,
- time-of-flight systems,
- frequency-modulated continuous-wave systems,
- coherent receivers,
- optical hybrids,
- balanced detectors,
- and optical phased arrays.

The automotive LiDAR inventions are application-specific descendants of the broader III-V-on-SiC platform.

### 9.2 Co-packaged optics

The SiC support may function as:

- a thermal handle,
- an optical alignment reference,
- a rigid optical-engine base,
- a low-expansion package platform,
- and a support adjacent to a switch ASIC.

### 9.3 SWIR imaging

An InGaAs/InP detector stack may be transferred through a thin SiC layer onto a silicon CMOS readout integrated circuit to form a fine-pixel detector array.

### 9.4 Quantum photonics and QKD

Embodiments may integrate:

- III-V single-photon sources,
- SPADs,
- waveguides,
- graphene functions,
- and vertically integrated optical/electrical connections.

### 9.5 Space multi-junction photovoltaics

A transferred III-V multi-junction stack may be supported by SiC instead of a thick germanium substrate. Transparent-conductor embodiments may include graphene, ultrathin metal meshes, doped transparent layers, or III-V contact layers.

## 10. Relationship to adjacent Cools IP platforms

### Thermally Active Photonic Substrate

Covers alumina-based buried-insulator layers and thermally conductive handlers that combine optical cladding and thermal transport.

### CPO Zero Thermal Budget Bonding

Covers low-thermal-budget joining and process sequencing for heterogeneous photonic integration.

### Transmissive Self-Aligned Annealing

Covers selective absorption and localized activation through a low-absorption optical path.

### Package-Substrate-Less SystemBoard

Covers transferred fine-line redistribution structures and elimination of the intermediate package substrate.

The combined platform can therefore include:

```text
InP-based active optical thin film
+ alumina-based optical/thermal buried insulator
+ SiC/RBSC thermally active support
+ transferred electrical/optical redistribution overlay
```
